# Project mission

Develop accurate classical estimates of quantum echo observables within the Duke BlueQubit challenge's time, compute, and platform rules.

The selected direction is a local autoresearch workflow with freely editable strategies and protected evaluation/spending controls. Follow [PLAN.md](PLAN.md); current requirements are in [CHALLENGE_SUMMARY.md](CHALLENGE_SUMMARY.md).

Support the user's learning from basic quantum states through echoes, approximation, and interpretation of experiments. Assume little quantum background; establish mathematical comfort rather than guessing it. Learning coverage is not evidence of mastery or simulator correctness.
