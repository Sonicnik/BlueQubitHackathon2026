Reading a Z signal

This sheet supports practice; it is not a record of demonstrated mastery.

P(0) + P(1) = 1
⟨Z⟩ = P(0) − P(1)
P(0) = (1 + ⟨Z⟩) / 2

⟨Z⟩ = +1: always outcome 0.
⟨Z⟩ = 0: outcomes equally likely.
⟨Z⟩ = −1: always outcome 1.

An individual Z outcome is +1 or −1. Its expectation is the probability-weighted average. A finite sample average can differ from that expectation.

For a pure one-qubit state with amplitudes α and β: P(0)=|α|², P(1)=|β|². These probabilities describe standard-basis measurement.

Sources: IBM quantum states, measurements, and Pauli matrices; IBM expectation value reference.
